💼 As an expert in the intersection between visual and computational cultures, I am available for consulting in the broad field of computational creativity (Creative AI), with a focus on screen and interactive media: film, TV, video platforms and video games.

## How I can help your organisation

- Expert analysis on the impacts and implications of AI for your organisation, including horizon scanning, forecasting and trend spotting for possible impacts to your business model
- Technical guidance on the capabilities and limitations of generative AI models, with a focus on large vision and language models (LVMs), text-to-image/video, image-to-image/video, motion and sound generation workflows
- Strategic planning for organisations seeking to integrate LVMs into their creative processes, especially those with large or growing visual assets, e.g. image and video collections
- Training and workshops on computational creativity, generative AI, and machine learning for creative professionals, including visual programming tools such as ComfyUI.
- Technical guidance on drafting tender documents, lists of requirements, technical schedules, statements of work, role profiles for recruitment, etc.

## Why work with me

As a scholar-practitioner in the field of AI and media, I bring a unique combination of academic rigour, industry knowledge, and technical creativity to consulting engagements. I have a decade of experience working with media and cultural organisation in the private and public sectors, including the BBC, the British Council, the British Film Institute, and Televisa. My research and teaching experience has also equipped me with the skills to analyse technological innovations in their broader cultural environments, and to communicate effectively with software engineers, designers, creative technologists, and business developers. Bridging technical, creative and strategic streams is one the core ability that I bring to consulting.
