The [AI and Cultural Production Workshop](https://www.scm.cityu.edu.hk/events/ai-and-cultural-production-workshop-2) was held **4 June 2026** at the Run Run Shaw Creative Media Centre, hosted by the [School of Creative Media, City University of Hong Kong](https://www.scm.cityu.edu.hk/). I was invited by [Espen Aarseth](https://www.scm.cityu.edu.hk/en/people/aarseth-espen) and [Sangjoon Lee](https://www.scm.cityu.edu.hk/people/lee-sangjoon), who were wonderful hosts throughout my visit.

## Computational Models of Cinematic Time in the Age of AI Video

My talk drew on my ongoing research on AI video generation and its implications for moving image culture, examining how cinematic time can be reconceptualised "not as a timeline but as a topology."
