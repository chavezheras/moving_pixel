I was also a guest in the radio show "Diseño Invisible" (invisible design). I'll post the recording here when it becomes available.

It was a pleasure to visit Ibero and to present the book in [Mexico City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico_City). I lived in CDMX for several years before moving to the UK, and although I visit often, I hadn't really been back for academic activities. It was a treat to be able to present in Spanish and connect with colleagues working in Mexico.  Many thanks to my colleague Miguel Rubio for the invitation and for the warm welcoming ¡Nos vemos pronto!
