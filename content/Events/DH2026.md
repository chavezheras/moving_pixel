[DH2026](https://dh2026.adho.org/), the 36th annual conference of the Alliance of Digital Humanities Organizations (ADHO), was held **27–31 July 2026** at the Daejeon Convention Center in Daejeon, South Korea, hosted by the Korean Association for Digital Humanities (KADH).

## AVinDH workshop
I co-organised the [AudioVisual & Digital Humanities workshop](https://avindh.org/annual-adho-conference-workshops/2026/), marking the 10th anniversary of the AVinDH Special Interest Group, together with [Mila Oiva](https://www.fau.eu/2026/03/news/new-to-fau-prof-dr-mila-oiva/) and colleagues from the SIG. The day brought together a reflective panel, lightning talks on current research, and a tool demonstration session on the state and future of working with audiovisual materials in the Digital Humanities.

During the Tool Cavalcade session I presented [[FrameSense]], our open-source CLI tool for processing large moving image collections into machine-readable datasets.

## ISSA poster
Also at this conference, a poster on the first [[ISSA|ISSA]] prototype, [MVP1 Segment](https://github.com/kingsdigitallab/issa/tree/main/prototypes/segment), developed by [Miguel Vieira](https://kdl.kcl.ac.uk/about/people/miguel-vieira/). The [full poster is available here](https://github.com/kingsdigitallab/issa/blob/main/prototypes/segment/etc/dh2026/poster.pdf).
