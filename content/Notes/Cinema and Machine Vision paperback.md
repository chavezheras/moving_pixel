The paperback edition of [*Cinema and Machine Vision: Artificial Intelligence, Aesthetics and Spectatorship*](https://edinburghuniversitypress.com/book-cinema-and-machine-vision.html) is now out with [Edinburgh University Press](https://edinburghuniversitypress.com/).

[add image of paperback here]

The book was first published in hardback and ebook in 2024. In its first year it accumulated 20 citations on Google Scholar, and is now held in over 260 libraries worldwide. Translations into Spanish and Chinese are in the works.

If you missed the hardback — or would like an affordable copy to lend, annotate, or press into someone's hands — this is your moment. [[Notes/book|You can also still win one here 🎲]]

> *Cinema and Machine Vision* unfolds the aesthetic, epistemic, and ideological dimensions of machine-seeing films and television using computers. With its critical-technical approach, this book presents to the reader key new problems that arise as AI becomes integral to visual culture.

The book launched at [KCL's AI Festival 2024](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/ai) to a packed room of 90+ people, was recorded as an episode of the [Video Essay Podcast](https://thevideoessay.com/) with [Will DiGravio](https://www.willdigravio.com/), and had a second launch at [Universidad Iberoamericana](https://ibero.mx/) in Mexico City in 2025, including a live radio interview broadcast nationally.
