## Main research themes and interests

![[assets/images/research_1.jpg|450]]

Most of my work falls in one of these overlapping categories:
- Computational moving image studies
- Computational creativity and creative computing
- Interdisciplinary design
- Synthetic media and generative approaches in computational humanities
- [Critical technical practice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critical_technical_practice), systems literacy, and computing cultures

At King's, I am affiliated to the following groups:
- [Creative AI Lab](https://creative-ai.org/)
- [Computational Humanities Research Group](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/research/computational-humanities-research-group)
- [AI Institute](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/ai)
- [Institute for Human and Synthetic Minds](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/news/kings-draws-together-expertise-to-make-the-world-healthier-and-advance-our-understanding-of-intelligence)

## Recent projects and outputs
- [[Projects/ISSA|Intelligent Systems for Screen Archives (ISSA)]] — a BFI-funded project developing AI tools and skills for moving image archives, in partnership with five UK archives
- [[Projects/DEERIN|DEERIN prototypes]] — four modular, open-source tools for data enrichment, exploration, retrieval, and interaction with screen heritage collections, developed through ISSA
- [[Projects/FrameSense|FrameSense]] — an open-source tool for processing large moving image collections into machine-readable datasets, developed with King's Digital Lab
- [[Notes/MESH Hub|MESH Hub]] — new Arts & Humanities infrastructure for computational media experimentation at King's, funded through a Research Infrastructure Fund bid
- [[Projects/ARCAM-net|ARCAM-net]] — a proposed European research network for computational audiovisual media, heading towards a 2027 COST Action resubmission led by Mila Oiva
- My book [Cinema and Machine Vision](https://edinburghuniversitypress.com/book-cinema-and-machine-vision.html) (Edinburgh University Press, 2024)
