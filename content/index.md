Welcome to my website, I am an academic and technologist specialised in the computational production and analysis of visual culture.

I am a Senior Lecturer (≈Associate Professor) in Computational Humanities and AI Media at [King's College London](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/people/daniel-chavez-heras)  where I am part of the [Computational Humanities Group](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/research/computational-humanities-research-group), the [Creative AI Lab](https://creative-ai.org/info) (in partnership with the [Serpentine Galleries](https://www.serpentinegalleries.org/))and an affiliate of [King's AI Institute](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/ai) which recently did [a profile of my work](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/meet-dr-daniel-chavez-heras) in their spotlight series. I also over a decade of experience in the cultural and creative industries with a focus on the screen sector, including projects and collaborations with the British Film Institute, the BBC, and the British Council.

My work combines critical frameworks in the history and theories of cinema, television, and photography, with advanced technical practice in creative and scientific computing, including applied machine learning technologies (AI). I aim to understand screen culture as a complex dynamic system, drawing on computational modelling and simulation techniques from complexity science.
